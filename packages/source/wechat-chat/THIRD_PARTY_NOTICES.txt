scripts/local/macos-memory.c and macos-debugger.plist come unchanged from weread-skill's macOS backend
(commit f629ca9, contributed by spdc-elm): it ports this project's authenticated codec traversal to Mach APIs.
lib/macos.mjs ports that backend's snapshot.py and macos_image.py (SQLCipher pages, committed WAL, V1/V2 image
cache) to Node.

Chat media format references (the local parser and key handling are implemented here):
- WeChat V1/V2 image layout and ASCII image-key discovery:
  https://github.com/ylytdeng/wechat-decrypt
- WeChat 4.1 account-seed image-key derivation:
  https://github.com/CN-Grace/Wechat-Emoticon-Parser
- WXGF / WXAM HEVC stream structure:
  https://sarv.blog/posts/wxam/

Runtime media dependencies are installed by npm, not copied into source:
- silk-wasm 3.7.1 (MIT): https://github.com/idranme/silk-wasm
- ffmpeg-static 5.3.0 (GPL-3.0-or-later): https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static
  The bundled FFmpeg binary carries its own license and build/source information
  in the package's accompanying files and upstream binary release.
